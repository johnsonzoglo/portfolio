03 / PRIVACY

Privacy policy

We collect information needed to operate your account and fulfil purchases: name, email, phone, delivery address, saved items, course progress, reviews, support messages, order details, and limited page-event analytics. Information is used for account access, fulfilment, learning progress, receipts, support, fraud prevention, and optional marketing you select. We do not sell personal information. Service providers receive only the data needed to deliver payments, email, hosting, or storage. 04 / REFUNDS

Refund policy

For physical products, contact support within 7 days of delivery if an item is defective, materially different from its listing, or damaged in transit. Return eligibility requires the item and included accessories to be returned in substantially the same condition, unless the defect prevents that.

For digital courses, request a refund within 7 days of purchase and before completing more than 20% of the course or downloading substantial resources. Approved refunds remove course access. Processing time depends on the original payment provider. Custom work, completed services, and clearly marked final-sale products are non-refundable unless required by applicable law.
